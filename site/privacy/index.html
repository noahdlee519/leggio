The short version

  • Leggio translates text on your device, using the translation models built into Chrome. The text you translate is not sent to us or to anyone else.
  • Your settings and the words you save are kept in your browser, on your computer.
  • The Leggio extension has no accounts, no servers and no analytics. We don’t collect, sell or share your data.
  • This website counts visits with anonymous, cookie-free analytics. See This website.

Text you translate

When you ask Leggio to translate a word or passage, Leggio passes that text to Chrome’s built-in Translator and Language Detector. Chrome runs the translation on your own computer. Leggio does not send the text, the page it came from or the page’s address to any server, ours or anyone else’s.

The same goes for PDFs: Leggio doesn’t upload the documents you read.

What Leggio stores

Leggio keeps two kinds of information, both in Chrome’s extension storage on your computer:

  • Your settings, such as the languages you read and translate into, and your preferences.
  • Your saved words: your vocabulary list, meaning the words and phrases you choose to save and any details saved along with them.

This information never leaves your device. It isn’t synced to an account, and we can’t see it.

What Leggio doesn’t collect

Leggio doesn’t collect any of the following:

  • Your name, email address or any other account details. There are no accounts.
  • Your browsing history or the addresses of pages you visit.
  • The contents of pages or documents you read.
  • Your location.
  • Analytics, usage statistics, crash reports or any other telemetry.
  • Advertising identifiers. Leggio shows no ads.

Chrome’s translation models

The first time you translate between a pair of languages, Chrome may need to download a translation model. Chrome handles that download itself, as it does for its other built-in features. The text you’re reading is not part of that request. Chrome’s own data practices are covered by Google’s privacy policy.

Permissions

The permissions Leggio requests are listed on its Chrome Web Store page, and Chrome shows them to you when you install it. Leggio uses them only to translate the text you select and to store your settings and saved words.

Sharing and selling

We don’t sell, rent or share your data, and we don’t use it for advertising, for creditworthiness or for any purpose unrelated to Leggio’s translation features. Because Leggio never sends your data to us, we have nothing to share.

Leggio’s use of information complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Deleting your data

Everything Leggio stores is on your computer, so you’re in control of it. Removing Leggio from Chrome (at chrome://extensions) deletes its settings and your saved words.

Children

Leggio is a general-audience tool. It doesn’t collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

This website

This website doesn’t use cookies or ads, and it loads everything, fonts included, from its own domain. The demo on the home page runs entirely in your browser and doesn’t save anything.

To see how many people visit and which pages they read, the website uses Vercel Web Analytics, from our hosting provider, Vercel. When you open a page, it records the page you viewed, the site that linked you here, your country or region, and your browser, operating system and device type. It doesn’t use cookies, and what it records isn’t tied to you or to your IP address. To count visitors, it uses an anonymous identifier that resets every day, so your visits can’t be linked from one day to the next or to other websites. We only look at the totals. This applies to the website only. The Leggio extension has no analytics.

Our hosting provider may also keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses and request times, to keep the site secure and running.

Changes to this policy

If we change how Leggio handles data, we’ll update this page and the effective date at the top before the change takes effect.
