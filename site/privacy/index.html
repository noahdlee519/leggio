The short version

  • Leggio translates text on your device, using the translation models built into Chrome. The text you translate is not sent to us, or to anyone else unless you choose to connect your own DeepL or Google Cloud account.
  • The words you save are kept in your browser, on your computer. So are your settings, which Chrome syncs to your other computers if you use Chrome sync.
  • Leggio has no accounts, no servers and no analytics. We don’t collect, sell or share your data.

Text you translate

When you select a word or passage and press Leggio’s key, Leggio reads that text and the sentence around it and passes them to Chrome’s built-in Translator and Language Detector, or to a small language detector bundled with Leggio. Both run on your own computer. Leggio does not send the text, the page it came from or the page’s address to any server, ours or anyone else’s.

On web pages, Leggio runs a small script that only listens for your translate and save keys. It doesn’t read or record the page, and it does nothing until you press one of them with text selected. You can turn it off in Leggio’s settings.

The same goes for PDFs: Leggio opens them in its own reader and doesn’t upload the documents you read.

Optional translation services

If you’d rather use DeepL or Google Cloud Translation, you can add your own API key for it in Leggio’s settings and choose that service. From then on, the words you look up or save, and the sentence they’re in, are sent to that service under your account. Its terms and privacy policy apply (DeepL, Google), and it may bill your account for the characters translated.

Your keys are stored only on your computer, aren’t synced, and are only ever sent to their own service. Nothing goes to either service unless you set this up.

What Leggio stores

Leggio keeps this information in Chrome’s extension storage:

  • Your settings, such as the languages you read and translate into, and your preferences. If you use Chrome sync, Chrome syncs them to your other computers through your Google account.
  • Your saved words: the words, phrases and sentences you choose to save, their meaning and sentence, and where you found them (the PDF’s name and page, or the web page’s title and address). They stay on this computer and aren’t synced.
  • API keys for DeepL or Google Cloud, if you add any. They stay on this computer and aren’t synced.
  • Text recognition results for scanned PDFs, kept so a document you reopen doesn’t need reading again.

None of it is sent to us, and we can’t see it.

What Leggio doesn’t collect

Leggio doesn’t collect any of the following:

  • Your name, email address or any other account details. There are no accounts.
  • Your browsing history or the addresses of pages you visit. (When you save a word from a web page, that page’s address is kept with the word, on your computer only.)
  • The contents of pages or documents you read.
  • Your location.
  • Analytics, usage statistics, crash reports or any other telemetry.
  • Advertising identifiers. Leggio shows no ads.

Chrome’s translation models

The first time you translate between a pair of languages, Chrome may need to download a translation model. Chrome handles that download itself, as it does for its other built-in features. The text you’re reading is not part of that request. Chrome’s own data practices are covered by Google’s privacy policy.

Scanned PDFs

Text recognition for scanned pages runs on your computer. The first time Leggio reads a scanned page in a language, it downloads that language’s recognition data (a few megabytes) from cdn.jsdelivr.net, a public content network. No part of your document is sent in that request.

Permissions

The permissions Leggio requests are listed on its Chrome Web Store page, and Chrome shows them to you when you install it. Leggio uses them only to translate the text you select, to open PDFs in its own reader, and to store your settings and saved words.

Sharing and selling

We don’t sell, rent or share your data, and we don’t use it for advertising, for creditworthiness or for any purpose unrelated to Leggio’s translation features. Because Leggio never sends your data to us, we have nothing to share.

Leggio’s use of information complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Deleting your data

Everything Leggio stores is on your computer, so you’re in control of it. Removing Leggio from Chrome (at chrome://extensions) deletes its settings, your saved words, any API keys and its text recognition results. You can also delete saved words one at a time, or all the words from one PDF or page, in Leggio’s word list.

Children

Leggio is a general-audience tool. It doesn’t collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

This website

This website doesn’t use cookies, analytics or third-party scripts, and it serves its fonts from its own server. The demo on the home page runs entirely in your browser and doesn’t save anything. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses and request times, to keep the site secure and running.

Changes to this policy

If we change how Leggio handles data, we’ll update this page and the effective date at the top before the change takes effect.
