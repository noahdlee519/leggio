Translations and meanings

Leggio shows translations from Chrome’s built-in translator, or from DeepL or Google Cloud if you add your own key. The meanings of idioms come from Wiktionary. All of these can be wrong, out of date or miss the sense a word has in your sentence. Leggio is a reading aid, not a professional translator or a dictionary you should cite.
